  - `إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ كَانَ سَمِيعًۢا بَصِيرًۭا` (inna'llāha kāna samī‘an baṣīrā): Indeed, Allah is All-Hearing (of every litigant's plea) and All-Seeing (of every hidden bias or corrupt appointment).
